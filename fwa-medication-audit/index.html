FWA Medication Claim Review

Compare a billed medication claim with the SFDA public price list and the prescription as written.

Audit aid only. Results are review flags, not findings of fraud, waste or abuse, and not clinical advice. This tool does not diagnose, prescribe, or judge whether a regimen is clinically appropriate. Refer clinical questions to a clinician or pharmacist.
Privacy: everything runs inside this browser tab. No claim data is sent anywhere. Claim details are not saved: they are lost when you reload or close the page. An imported price list is kept only if you tick “Remember this price list” (stored in this browser on this device only).

Official source data 1. SFDA price list

How to get the SFDA list
  1. Open the SFDA Drugs List: sfda.gov.sa/en/drugs-list (on the SFDA site, also reachable from the “Information Lists” menu, then “Drugs”).
  2. Download the list (SFDA provides it as an Excel file). Note the update date shown by SFDA, or the date you downloaded it.
  3. Open it in Excel or LibreOffice and choose Save As → CSV UTF-8 (comma delimited).
  4. Import the CSV below and check the column mapping.

This tool has no built-in SFDA data and does not download from SFDA automatically, so you always know which file and date the prices came from. Check the SFDA website for its terms of use.
